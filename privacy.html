Privacy Policyপ্রাইভেসি পলিসি

What we store

  • On this device: your game progress, settings, coins and any downloaded voice packs — kept only in your browser.
  • Online (Facebook): a player ID provided by Facebook (not your name or email) and your game backup, stored in Facebook Instant Games player data. We never ask for an email, phone number or password.
  • Problem reports: if the game crashes, the error message, game version and browser type — used only to fix bugs.
  • Purchases: in-game purchases are processed by Meta (Facebook Payments). We only receive confirmation of what was bought; we never see your card or payment details.
  • Analytics & ads: Ads are served by the Facebook Audience Network under Meta policies. Game usage statistics (for example which matches were played) are sent to Facebook Analytics and do not include your name or email.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices and data deletion

  • In the game open Settings › Delete my data. It permanently removes your progress, coins, settings and your online backup, on this device and in Facebook player data. This cannot be undone.
  • To change your ads/analytics consent at any time, clear this site's data in your browser.
  • You can also manage the ads you see and the data Facebook holds about you in your Facebook settings.
